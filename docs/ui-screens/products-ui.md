# ChronosForge — Products & Inventory UI Prompt

Design a premium mobile **Products / Inventory** screen for **ChronosForge**, an AI-powered retail operations platform.

## Style
- Clean modern enterprise UI
- Minimal SaaS design
- Premium light theme
- Soft shadows and rounded cards
- Professional AI-assisted inventory feel
- Use Inter font

## Colors
- Primary: `#2563EB`
- Background: `#F8FAFC`
- Text: `#0F172A`
- Success: `#16A34A`
- Warning: `#EA580C`
- Danger: `#DC2626`
- Border: `#E2E8F0`

## Layout

### Header
- Title: **"Products"**
- Subtitle: **"Inventory & AI Pricing"**

### Search
- Rounded modern search bar
- Minimal clean style

### Filter Chips
Horizontal selectable chips:
- All
- Low Stock
- AI Updated
- High Demand

### Product List
Modern product cards showing:
- Product image
- Product name
- SKU
- Stock quantity
- Current price
- Region

### Product States
- Color-coded stock indicators
- Low stock warning highlights
- AI Updated badge on relevant products

### Expandable Details
Expandable section inside cards showing:
- Previous Price vs New Price
- AI pricing reasoning
- Optimization summary

### Floating Action Button
- Circular floating `+` button
- Modern elevated design

## Requirements
- Mobile-first layout
- Spacious spacing and clean hierarchy
- Smooth rounded corners
- Premium typography
- Intelligent AI inventory management feel
- Highly polished professional SaaS aesthetic
